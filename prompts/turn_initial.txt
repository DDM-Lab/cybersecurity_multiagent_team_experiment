Attack categories in this study:
- DoS: flooding a host or service with traffic to exhaust it.
- Evasion: scanning or attack traffic disguised to avoid detection, for example slow scans, decoy or forged source addresses, or fragmented packets.
- Exfiltration: moving data out of the network to an outside host.
- SMB: spreading between internal machines over Windows file sharing (SMB).
- Recon: scanning or mapping hosts and services.
- DVWA: attacks on a web application, such as SQL injection, command injection or file inclusion.
- C&C beaconing: a compromised machine checking in with a command and control server at regular intervals.
- Credential harvesting: attempts to steal or guess usernames and passwords.
